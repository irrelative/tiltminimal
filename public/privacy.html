Privacy

Tilt Minimal records page-view totals, which tables are played, when games start and finish, active playing time, and completed scores. These help improve the game. The statistics dashboard is private.

There are no analytics cookies, accounts, advertising trackers, or persistent visitor identifiers. Each game gets a random ID to avoid counting its result twice. Our analytics database does not store IP addresses, names, email addresses, or full browsing URLs. Cloudflare hosts the site and processes network information, including IP addresses, to serve requests and limit abuse.

Settings and personal high scores stay in your browser’s local storage. Online statistics are separate; clearing browser storage does not delete anonymous game records. View totals and game records are retained for historical comparison.
